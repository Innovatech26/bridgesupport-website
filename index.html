About BridgeSupport

Closing the Distance Between Talent & Technology

BridgeSupport Limited is a registered Nigerian company dedicated to building Africa's digital future. Founded by five industry professionals across Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt, we understand the unique challenges and opportunities in Nigeria's tech landscape.

Our mission: close the distance between great technology and the people who need it — and between skilled talent and the organisations that need them.
