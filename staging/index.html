Who We Are

Built to bridge gaps in Africa's digital economy

Incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act, 2020, BridgeSupport Limited exists to close the distance between great technology and the people who need it — and between skilled talent and the organisations that need them.

We are a private limited company headquartered in Nigeria, with a clear mandate: deliver technology services, build human capacity, support recruitment, and supply the tools that power modern businesses.
